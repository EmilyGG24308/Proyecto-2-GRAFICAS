Mikage Shrine Raytracer
A voxel raytraced diorama with two beautiful worlds
How to Explore:
Click to capture mouse / look around
WASD to move forward/left/right/backward
Space / Shift to move up/down
Scroll to zoom in/out
Q / E to rotate the scene
R to reset to start position
F12 for high-quality screenshot
✨ The Portal Portal
When you see the glowing portal, aim your crosshair at it and click to travel between worlds!

🏯 Shrine World: Golden-hour Japanese shrine with peaceful atmosphere
🌙 Spirit World: Red-light district with brilliant nighttime lanterns
Built with Rust + Custom Raytracer | Click to capture mouse | Esc to release
